About VPM College of Engineering ClubConnect

Vidya Prasarak Mandal's College of Engineering, Thane, Maharashtra, provides an active ecosystem of student-led clubs and extracurricular activities spanning technology, innovation, arts, culture, sports, leadership, and research.

Our Mission

To provide an equitable, transparent, and digitally-enabled platform where every student can discover, participate in, and lead extracurricular activities that complement academic learning and personal development.

Our Vision

To cultivate well-rounded engineering graduates equipped with technological competence, creativity, leadership skills, cultural awareness, teamwork, and a spirit of innovation.

How to Get Involved?

Any enrolled student at Vidya Prasarak Mandal's College of Engineering can register on ClubConnect, explore official student clubs, submit an application to join club committees, participate in extracurricular activities, and register for upcoming events, competitions, workshops, hackathons, tournaments, and seminars.
